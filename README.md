### Project Description
Flushverse is a satirical civilization simulator where every toilet on campus is treated as a full citizen — complete with its own economy, elections, sports league, dating app, and AI-powered personality you can actually chat with.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
Toilets are boring, voiceless, and criminally underrepresented in civic life. They flush, they sit there, they get ignored — no rights, no representation, no drama. Someone had to fix this injustice.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
We gave every toilet a personality, a bank account, and a vote. In Flushverse, toilets have their own currency (FlushCoins), run for political office, play in the Toilet Premier League, swipe on Toilet Tinder, and each one has a unique AI persona you can talk to — powered by their own zodiac sign and quirks (weak flush = anxious, high traffic = extroverted, and so on). When one breaks down, Captain Plunger gets dispatched to save the day.
